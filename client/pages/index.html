General information

> How does it work?

Every month, three brackets of 6 charts are selected for the score clash. The BEGINNER bracket is aimed at newer players, up to 3rd dan. The NORMAL bracket is for more experienced players, 4th to 8th dan. The HYPER bracket is aimed at players around 9th to chuuden. The ANOTHER bracket is aimed at chuuden and kaiden players.
If you are 4th dan or higher, you can only submit scores to the NORMAL bracket or higher. If you are 9th dan or higher, you can only submit scores to the HYPER or ANOTHER bracket. This is to ensure fair competition within each bracket.

Each bracket has 5 normal charts and 1 boss chart. The boss chart is an extra challenge that yields 2x the points of a normal chart. It is indicated in bold yellow in the score and chart table. The boss chart is harder to score on, but highly recommended to try anyway, as it can significantly boost your score.

Your score is calculated based on your placement among other players on the same chart. The formula for this is as follows:

rating = 100 * (1 - ((placement - 1) / total players))

100 points is the maximum score you can achieve on a chart. 0 points is the minimum score you can achieve on a chart (for example, if you haven't achieved any score). After that, the weighted average of the ratings of all charts is calculated, with the number of players per chart as the weight. Again, the boss chart is weighted double.

> How do I play? (Flower)

The cabinet must be registered on Flower in order to participate. If it is not registered on that server, you will have to use Kamaitachi.

Press the button at the top of this page to sign up with your Flower account.

If you are a new player, please follow the instructions on IIDX.org for beginner information on how to start the game and play one credit. This website has much more information on how to play the game, so please read it carefully. Ask your arcade operator or another player for help if you need assistance with registering your card on Flower. After registering your card on Flower, you can press the button at the top of this page and start playing in Accuracy Arena.

> How do I play? (Kamaitachi)

You must adhere to the Kamaitachi rules to participate.

Press the button at the top of this page to sign up with your Kamaitachi account.

Set up or use any of the supported score import methods. Your scores will be automatically fetched.

> How do I read the chart levels?

The difficulty scale in IIDX ranges from 1 to 12. IIDX has 5 different difficulty brackets: Beginner, Normal, Hyper, Another and Leggendaria. These are abbreviated as B, N, H, A and L respectively. There are also two different play modes: Single (7key+1) and Double (14key+2). These are abbreviated as SP and DP

Let's look at some examples:

  • SPA10. This stands for Single Play Another level 10.
  • DPN4, which stands for Double Play Normal level 4.
